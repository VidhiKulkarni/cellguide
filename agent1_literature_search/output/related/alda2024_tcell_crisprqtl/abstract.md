# Mapping the functional impact of non-coding regulatory elements in primary T cells through single-cell CRISPR screens

Alda-Catalinas C. et al., *Genome Biology* (2024). PMC10835965, DOI 10.1186/s13059-024-03176-z

Immune disease-associated genetic variants concentrate within T-cell-specific open chromatin regulatory regions, but translating them into functional gene knowledge remains hard. The authors built "primary T cell crisprQTL," a CRISPRi-based single-cell functional screening pipeline in primary human CD4+ T cells, perturbing 45 non-coding regulatory elements and 35 transcription start sites with ZIM3-dCas9 while profiling roughly 250,000 single-cell transcriptomes. A bespoke element-to-gene analytical pipeline recovers both previously annotated and novel regulatory links, and integrating genetic association data lets the platform nominate effector genes for GWAS loci -- a scalable route from T-cell-specific chromatin context to guide-level functional readout.
