CRISPR/Cas9-Based Modeling of JAK2 V617F Mutation in K562 Cells Reveals Enhanced Proliferation and Sensitivity to Therapeutic Agents
International Journal of Molecular Sciences (2025)
Nilsri N., Mekchaaum R., Kalasin S., Jongjitwimol J., Daowtak K.
PMC12111430, DOI 10.3390/ijms26104600

Abstract: The Janus kinase 2 (JAK2) protein fulfills an important role in hematopoiesis via the JAK/STAT pathway. The aim of the present study was to establish K562 cell lines with a point mutation in exon 14 (JAK2 p.V617F) using CRISPR/Cas9 RNP technology (Alt-R HiFi Cas9 + crRNA/tracrRNA, IDT design tool). Modified cells were confirmed by droplet digital PCR and Sanger sequencing, characterized for JAK2 expression and proliferation, and tested against IFN-α2a and arsenic trioxide. JAK2 V617F-mutated K562 clones showed increased JAK2 expression, faster proliferation, greater sensitivity to both therapeutic agents, and could differentiate into megakaryocyte-like cells upon PMA stimulation.

Methodology note relevant to CellGuide: standard RNP-based K562 editing workflow (Alt-R gRNA design, HiFi Cas9, nucleofection, DDPCR + Sanger validation) — useful as a K562-specific wet-lab reference if the hackathon team needs to validate guides experimentally in K562, distinct from Ito et al.'s T-cell-focused protocol.
