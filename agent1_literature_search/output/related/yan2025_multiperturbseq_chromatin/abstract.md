# Pooled CRISPR screens with joint single-nucleus chromatin accessibility and transcriptome profiling

Yan R.E. et al., *Nature Biotechnology* 43:1628-1634 (2025). PMID 39572737, DOI 10.1038/s41587-024-02475-x

MultiPerturb-seq is a high-throughput pooled CRISPR screening platform that jointly captures single-nucleus chromatin accessibility, the transcriptome, and guide RNA identity from the same cells, using combinatorial indexing combined with droplet microfluidics to scale throughput and integrate all three modalities. Applied to a rare pediatric cancer model, the platform identified key differentiation genes and nominated ZNHIT1 as a candidate target for cancer reprogramming therapy, demonstrating that linking a guide's perturbation effect directly to the chromatin state of its own target locus at single-cell resolution can recover regulatory relationships that bulk or single-modality screens miss.
