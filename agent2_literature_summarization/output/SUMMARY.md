## Cross-paper synthesis

**The corpus now converges on CellGuide's core premise: sequence-only guide scores do not transfer across cell contexts, and chromatin accessibility is the single most reusable context feature.** The strongest evidence is `cohen2026_context_determinants` — 1,005 endogenous SpCas9 sites across 8 cell systems *including primary human T cells and K562* — where DeepCRISPR's within-context Spearman of 0.96 collapses to 0.08 out-of-context and SPROUT's 0.41 falls to 0.11, with individual feature effects **flipping sign** between K562 and U937. `cucuy2026_tomato_chromatin_efficiency` reproduces this independently in a uniform non-human system (420 sgRNAs, matched ATAC-seq): open chromatin significantly raises cutting efficiency (p<0.005) while transcription does not, and human-trained Azimuth ranks guides at r=0.05. On the positive side, accessibility is *additive and cheap*: `amirabad2025_cas12_chromatin_foundation` lifts Cas12a on-target Spearman 0.76→0.78 by fusing a single binary ATAC label onto a sequence encoder, and `srikanth2026_crispri_library_design` (Doench lab) builds ATAC-peak overlap directly into its RS3i CRISPRi score, where 81.6% of guides scoring >1.2 are active versus 14.7% below 0. Together these bound the realistic gain from an accessibility term — real and consistent in sign, but modest (a few Spearman points) when sequence features are already strong, and much larger when the model is applied out-of-context.

**Chromatin cuts both ways, which is what makes a two-term (efficiency × specificity) metric necessary rather than optional.** `feng2026_egold_chromatin_offtarget` is the key result here: holding guide sequence constant across sequence-identical off-target sites and calling 2,145,592 off-target events by 40× WGS, off-target editing is significantly *more* frequent in open chromatin and is suppressed by DNA methylation and H3K9me3, with chromatin dependence growing as mismatch count rises. So the same accessibility signal that raises on-target efficiency also raises off-target risk — a naive "prefer open chromatin" heuristic would silently degrade specificity. `joyce2026_pocketseq_dcas9krab` shows the failure mode concretely for CRISPRi: measured dCas9-KRAB binding sites overlap CRISPOR's mismatch-based predictions at 1/334 and 0/47 sites, and the actual phenotype attributed to *SOCS3* traced to an off-target at *PEX19*. `schep2024_chromatin_drugs` adds that chromatin state governs not just efficiency but NHEJ:MMEJ repair-pathway balance. Practical off-target calibration for Agent 3 comes from `drepanos2025_ontarget_offtarget_library`: GUIDE-seq activity by seed-distal-region mismatch count is 100/43/5.7/0.4% for 0/1/2/3 mismatches, and an aggregate-CFD threshold of 4.8 reaches F1 0.73 — a directly implementable specificity prior, to be modulated by (not replaced with) the chromatin term.

**Cross-context and guide-level variance are large enough to dominate.** `feng2024_crispri_hpsc_map` (34 iPSC lines, ~2M cells, 7,226 genes) finds guide identity to be the *top* source of variance — 24-fold higher than donor genetic background — with only 60% of knockdowns significant and 4/19 lines non-responsive; it also confirms 112/2,067 predicted off-target *trans* effects. `zhang2025_crispri_k562_screens` quantifies the floor in K562, the Ito et al. comparator line: only 4/18 sgRNAs achieve log2FC < −1, and public datasets run 97/240 and 56/377 effective. `chardon2024_crispra_celltype_elements` shows the same cell-type restriction on the activation side (K562 vs iPSC-derived neurons), and `kristof2025_crispri_repressors` shows effector potency itself is context-dependent (MAX-containing fusions potent in HEK293T, failing in A549/HCT116/HeLa via cofactor availability). This is the variance a context-aware metric exists to explain.

**Coverage gaps and cautions for Agent 3.** (i) The CRISPRi/CRISPRa gap flagged in the previous pass is now closed — 15+ CRISPRi/CRISPRa papers are present — but the *modalities are not interchangeable*: CRISPRi failure modes are promoter/TSS-architecture-driven (`ni2026_crispri_casrx_context`: sgATF5 co-represses the overlapping-TSS *NUP62* by 99.3%, which no spacer-sequence score would catch) rather than mismatch-driven, so a single specificity term cannot serve both Cas9-nuclease and CRISPRi without a modality switch. (ii) Two papers are bacterial-only (`kiattisewee2025_bacterial_crispra_rules`, `xiang2025_glide_crispri_design`) and carry no chromatin component — useful as positional/seed-rule templates, not as chromatin evidence. (iii) `arvidsson2025_crispra_sgrna_screen` is a useful **negative** result bounding sequence-only CRISPRa features: neither TSS distance nor nearby TF binding sites correlated with activation. (iv) Ito et al. 2024 still contributes **no off-target data**, so the specificity term must be sourced externally — `drepanos2025`, `feng2026_egold`, `ozden2024_crispai_uncertainty` and `sari2025_crisprbert` are the candidates. (v) Ten of the highest-signal entries are 2025–2026 bioRxiv preprints (`cohen2026`, `feng2026`, `srikanth2026`, `ni2026`, `wang2026`, `zhang2025`, `joyce2026`, `cucuy2026`, `drepanos2025`, `taifour2026`) — not peer-reviewed; weight metric parameters accordingly, and note `joyce2026`'s CRISPOR comparison rests on only 4 guides.

## Notes / confidence flags

- **Preprint weighting.** `cohen2026`, `feng2026`, `srikanth2026`, `ni2026`, `wang2026`, `zhang2025`, `joyce2026`, `cucuy2026`, `drepanos2025`, `taifour2026`, `kiattisewee2025` are 2025–2026 bioRxiv preprints — not peer-reviewed.
- **`joyce2026_pocketseq_dcas9krab`** is a short-format preprint; its CRISPOR-vs-measured comparison rests on 4 guides. It motivates chromatin-aware specificity scoring but does not size the effect.
- **Partial texts.** `feng2026_egold_chromatin_offtarget`, `kristof2025_crispri_repressors`, and `huang2024_dcas9_tcell_epigenetic` were rebuilt from `sections/` after a Paperclip slab outage — load-bearing sections present, some Methods subsections missing. One `feng2026` results subsection on open chromatin is unretrievable (filename `/` breaks path parsing); its conclusion is covered via the Discussion.
- **Abstract-only extractions.** `alda2024`, `chardon2024`, `malekos2024`, `mu2024`, `yan2025` plus the 12 pre-existing abstract-only slugs were extracted at abstract depth. `yan2025_multiperturbseq_chromatin` does not state its CRISPR modality or cell line; `mu2024_launch_epigenome_ml` does not name its cell lines. Full-text fetch would be needed for feature-level use.
- **`schmitz2025_uncertainty_guide_selection`**: data type for field (4) remains underdetermined from the abstract alone (only "mouse genome coverage" stated).
- **Ito et al. 2024 still has no off-target/specificity data** — the specificity term must come from elsewhere. Best candidates: `drepanos2025_ontarget_offtarget_library` (GUIDE-seq mismatch calibration + CFD threshold), `feng2026_egold_chromatin_offtarget` (chromatin-modulated off-target), `ozden2024_crispai_uncertainty` and `sari2025_crisprbert` (both CHANGE-seq primary-T-cell-trained).
- **Non-mammalian entries.** `kiattisewee2025` and `xiang2025` are bacterial (no chromatin); `cucuy2026` is plant. Their design rules transfer only as templates, not as parameter values — though `cucuy2026`'s open-vs-closed chromatin result is mechanistically relevant precisely because it is in a different genome.
- **Modality mismatch caution.** CRISPRi off-target failure is dominated by promoter/TSS architecture (`ni2026`, `joyce2026`) and CRISPRi tolerates far shorter spacers than nuclease KO (`moore2025`: repression at 9 nt vs KO failing below 17 nt). A Cas9-nuclease specificity term should not be reused unmodified for CRISPRi.
