# Agent 2 structured extraction — amirabad2025_cas12_chromatin_foundation

1. **Experimental design**: Computational / machine-learning only (no new wet-lab experiments). The authors ("Cas-FM") feed frozen pre-trained foundation-model embeddings of the gRNA — RNA-FM (transcriptome-trained, 640-dim T12 embeddings) or DNABERT-2 (genomic DNA) — into the DeepCpf1 CNN regression head to predict CRISPR-Cas12a (Cpf1) on-target cleavage activity, then add a second modality: a binary ATAC-seq chromatin-accessibility (CA) label per guide, projected by an MLP and combined with the sequence features by element-wise multiplication before the regression head. Trained 100 epochs, Adam, lr 5e-5, batch 32, MSE loss, evaluated by Spearman rank correlation. The underlying measurements are re-used from Kim et al. 2018 (DeepCpf1 HT1 lentiviral integrated synthetic guide-target library, indel frequency by deep sequencing).
2. **Target genes**: No endogenous gene panel — the training data are ~15,000 synthetic 20-nt gRNA / matched-target constructs (HT1-1, n = 15,000 for train/validation; HT1-2, n = 1,290 for test) integrated as barcoded reporter cassettes, with 20-nt, 34-bp, and 50-bp sequence-context representations per guide. Gene symbols are not named.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — Spearman correlation on the held-out test set. Sequence-only Cas-FM (RNA-FM) reached 0.76, beating DeepCpf1 (0.71, itself one-hot + chromatin accessibility), Boosted RT 0.66, Lasso 0.64, L1L2 0.64, L2 0.63, CINDEL 0.61; Cas-FM (DNABERT-2) badly underperformed at 0.49. Adding the binary chromatin-accessibility modality raised Cas-FM-CA (RNA-FM) to 0.78. A 34-nt context window beat both 20-nt and 50-nt windows. Appendix Fig. 3 reports that gRNAs targeting accessible regions had significantly higher measured activity than those in inaccessible regions, and that continuous ATAC-seq signal intensity correlates with gRNA activity in both train and test splits. Off-target — not addressed; the paper predicts on-target activity only and does not measure or model off-target sites (off-target/safety is mentioned once in the introduction as motivation, citing Doench et al. 2016).
4. **Data type**: Human cell lines only, and only indirectly — the gRNA activity data come from HEK293T cells (lentivirally integrated synthetic target library, Kim et al. 2018), and the chromatin accessibility data are public HEK293T ATAC-seq (GEO GSM2902624, files HEK293_ATAC_medium_depth_bio2_tech1.1/.2.fastq.gz). No primary cells, animal model, or tissue.

**Relevance flag**: Highly relevant and the most directly reusable of the three — it is a concrete demonstration that chromatin accessibility is orthogonal to even strong sequence representations for on-target guide efficiency (Spearman 0.76 -> 0.78), and it documents a fully specified ATAC-seq -> per-guide accessibility pipeline we can copy for the efficiency term: Bowtie2 to hg38, MACS2 narrow-peak calling, normalized-signal cutoff of 0.001, BLAT the guide to its best-matching locus, convert to BED, `bedtools intersect` with peaks, assign binary accessible (1) / inaccessible (0). Caveats for our use: it is Cas12a rather than SpCas9, the "context" is a single cell line (HEK293T, same line for guides and ATAC) so it offers no cross-cell-type transferability evidence, and it contributes nothing to a specificity/off-target term.
