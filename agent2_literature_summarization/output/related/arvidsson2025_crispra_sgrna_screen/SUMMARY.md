# Agent 2 structured extraction — arvidsson2025_crispra_sgrna_screen

1. **Experimental design**: CRISPRa. A plasmid-transfection fluorescence reporter screening assay in 96-well format: MiniCas9V2 (compact dead *S. aureus* Cas9 fused to a miniature VPR effector) plus U6-driven 22-nt sgRNA cassettes (pDPL0), a TdTomato reporter driven by the target gene's ~500-600 bp promoter, and a TagBFP2 transfection normalizer, co-transfected with PEI. A tailored "L3G0" Golden Gate workflow assembles up to eight validated sgRNA cassettes for downstream lentiviral (LV) or AAV packaging; top hits were re-validated for endogenous mRNA activation by RT-qPCR after transfection (lentiviral transduction of the same constructs failed to activate; data not shown).
2. **Target genes**: Three mouse genes relevant to neurodegenerative disease — *Tfeb*, *Adam17*, *Sirt1*. Seven sgRNA binding sites per promoter (in the 500-600 bp window immediately upstream of the TSS), screened as singles and pairwise duals (28 conditions per gene), plus a 22-nt scrambled (Scr) non-targeting control.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — reporter activation (TdTomato/TagBFP2 fold over Scr): *Tfeb* 7/28 conditions significant, max 3.96 +/- 0.10-fold (sgRNA 3+4); *Adam17* 12/28 significant, max 6.64 +/- 0.52-fold (sgRNA 5) and 6.66 +/- 0.39-fold (sgRNA 1+5); *Sirt1* 17/28 significant, max 5.13 +/- 0.47-fold (sgRNA 1+4). Fewer than 50% of single sgRNAs gave significant activation, and antagonistic dual-sgRNA interactions (two-way ANOVA + Combination Index) were more frequent than synergistic ones. Endogenous mRNA in Neuro2A: only *Sirt1* reached significance — sgRNA 1+3 gave 17.67 +/- 7.04-fold (about 4x higher than the reporter assay predicted) and sgRNA 1+4 about 10-fold; *Tfeb* sgRNA 4+3 gave a non-significant ~1.5-fold; *Adam17* showed no significant endogenous activation despite strong reporter activation — an explicit reporter-vs-endogenous discordance. Feature correlations largely failed: no correlation of activation with sgRNA distance from TSS or distance to nearest TFBS cluster for any gene; for *Sirt1* only, >50 TFBS overlapping the sgRNA binding site significantly lowered activation (Welch's t(25.2) = 2.9, p < 0.01) while more TFBS within 50 bp correlated positively (r(28) = 0.51, p < 0.01). CiiiDER predicted 251 / 71 / 145 TFBS in the *Tfeb* / *Adam17* / *Sirt1* promoters. Off-target — not measured. Off-target risk appears only as motivation (minimizing sgRNA number reduces possible off-target effects, recombination, and poor viral production); the scrambled control was BLAST-verified against the mouse genome, but no off-target assay or quantification was performed.
4. **Data type**: Two immortalized cell lines — HEK 293T (ATCC CRL-3216) for the plasmid reporter screen, and Neuro2A mouse neuroblastoma for endogenous mRNA validation. Targets are mouse promoter sequences. No primary cells, animal model, or tissue work.

**Relevance flag**: Moderately relevant as a cautionary/negative result for a chromatin-context-aware metric: purely promoter-sequence features (TSS distance, TFBS density/clusters) failed to predict CRISPRa efficacy, and the authors state explicitly that the plasmid-based assay "does not consider gene and cell-specific epigenetics," attributing the reporter-vs-endogenous discordance (strong *Adam17* reporter activation, no endogenous mRNA change) to differences in promoter accessibility and target copy number — direct support for an accessibility term over sequence-proximity heuristics. It supplies no ATAC/epigenetic measurements, no cross-cell-type transferability test, and no off-target data usable as a specificity term; it also uses SaCas9 rather than SpCas9.
