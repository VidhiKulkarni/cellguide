# Agent 2 structured extraction — cohen2026_context_determinants

1. **Experimental design**: SpCas9 cleavage/indel (knockout-style) profiling at **endogenous** loci — not knock-in, not CRISPRi/CRISPRa — paired with a large computational feature-engineering and modeling study. Delivery is mostly Cas9-gRNA **RNP by electroporation/nucleofection** (primary human T cells, K562, PLX placental stromal cells; tomato leaf protoplasts by PEG-RNP; prawn and black soldier fly embryos), with U937 instead using lentiviral lentiCas9-Blast plus lentiviral single-guide transduction, and tomato hairy roots using an Agrobacterium rhizogenes-delivered GFP-Cas9 construct. Readout is amplicon NGS (Illumina MiSeq 2x150) analyzed with CRISPResso2, plus PacBio long-read amplicon sequencing analyzed with CRISPECTOR for hairy roots.
2. **Target genes**: Not determinable as specific gene symbols from the saved text — no gene names are given. Scale: 1,297 endogenous target sites generated, 1,005 passing post-processing filters, spanning 8 cell systems at 59-187 sites per system.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — measured as endogenous editing efficiency (percent edited reads) per site. Headline result is the collapse of published sequence-only predictors on endogenous data: DeepCRISPR falls from Spearman 0.96 on its own dataset to a mean 0.08 on these human endogenous data; SPROUT falls from 0.41 to a mean 0.11 across other cell types; inter-model prediction agreement is only median rho 0.18-0.35 across the 8 systems. Regression ensembles trained on these data beat all public models in 7 of 8 cell systems (4 significantly); linear ensembles win in human cells, XGBoost/Random Forest in fly, tomato and prawn. Of 557 engineered features, determinants flip direction across cell types — PAM-adjacent local site density (local_sites_count_ngg_pam) is significantly higher among LOW-efficiency guides in K562 but POSITIVELY associated with efficiency in U937 and tomato leaf protoplasts; DNAshape minor groove width and Roll are positive predictors in U937 only; codon-usage features dominate in K562 with strong partial correlations after regressing out four public predictors (CAI_avg r = -0.42, chimera_avg r = -0.46, codon_freqs_avg r = -0.46, aa_freqs_avg r = -0.34, all p = 0.0001), while in T cells local_sites_count_ngg_pam_1000 and _10000 give r = -0.28 and -0.31 and Head3 gives r = +0.15. Repair outcomes, by contrast, are conserved: deletions outnumber insertions in every system (p < 1e-260), 1-bp insertions dominate the insertion spectrum, and the inserted base copies the nucleotide immediately upstream (position -4) of the cut (permutation p < 0.001 after FDR). Off-target — not quantified as a specificity metric. Off-target/near-cognate sites enter only as PREDICTIVE FEATURES (a "competition" family counting partially matching sites at 1 kb-100 kb scales and genome-wide, with and without NGG PAM); CRISPECTOR output was merged "for downstream analysis of on- and off-target editing events" and tomato off-target amplicons were designed via GoGenome, but no off-target rates are reported in the saved text.
4. **Data type**: Four human cell systems — **primary human T cells** (MACS-isolated, activated, RNP electroporation), **K562** erythroleukemia, **U937** pro-monocytic histiocytic lymphoma (differentiated to M2 macrophages), and **PLX** maternal/fetal placenta-derived mesenchymal-like adherent stromal cells; plus tomato (Solanum lycopersicum cv. M82) leaf protoplasts and hairy roots, giant river prawn (Macrobrachium rosenbergii) primary embryonic cell cultures, and black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens) embryos.

**Relevance flag**: The strongest direct evidence in this corpus that guide efficiency is cell-context-dependent and that sequence-only scores do not transfer — and it covers both of CellGuide's benchmark contexts (primary T cells and K562) with the same endogenous RNP assay. It supplies a concrete, human-only epigenetics/accessibility feature family to import into the efficiency term: DNase-seq hypersensitivity, CTCF boundary overlap, H3K4me3 peaks, RRBS methylation plus methylation-motif scores, and TADbit Hi-C 3D features (tad_density, tad_angle, tad_interactions at 100 kb resolution), with codon-usage-bias metrics offered as an accessibility proxy where ATAC/DNase data are unavailable. It also directly motivates a chromatin-aware specificity term: the discussion recommends modeling off-target/competition sites in a chromatin-aware manner because off-target sites embedded in closed chromatin are less likely to recruit Cas9 and therefore may not meaningfully compete with the intended target.
