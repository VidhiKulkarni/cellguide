# Agent 2 structured extraction — drepanos2025_ontarget_offtarget_library

1. **Experimental design**: SpCas9 CRISPR knockout (CRISPRko), predominantly computational library design with validating pooled screens. A new off-target "promiscuity" classifier (CRISPick Aggregate CFD) is trained on published tiling-screen depletion data and calibrated against a 114-guide GUIDE-seq corpus, then used to design the genome-wide library **Jacquere**; validation is by lentiviral pooled viability (dropout) screens using an all-in-one Cas9+sgRNA vector (pRDA_734) and, separately, split Cas9/guide delivery.
2. **Target genes**: Genome-wide — Jacquere targets 20,550 human protein-coding genes (union of RefSeq 08-2024, GENCODE 47, CHESS 3.1.3) with 60,617 unique guides at a quota of 3 guides/gene, plus 900 intergenic and 100 non-targeting controls; a mouse counterpart (Julianna) is also provided. Training/benchmarking used the DeWeirdt 2022 tiling set (201 essential genes / 48,742 guides; 198 nonessential genes / 33,643 guides). Named genes are limited to the paralog case study *FCGR2B* / *FCGR2C* (82.5% sequence similarity, forced to share a guide) and the critiqued external hit *Calhm2*.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — guides are ranked by Rule Set 3 (RS3) score with a preferred cutoff of RS3 >= 0.2. In A375 viability screens Jacquere separated essential from nonessential genes at ROC-AUC 0.96 vs 0.92 for Brunello (PR-AUC 0.98 vs 0.95); replicate Pearson r = 0.93 (A375) and 0.95 (A549). Of 130 common-essential genes Brunello failed to call at FDR<0.05, Jacquere recovered 97, and those genes' Jacquere guides had significantly higher RS3 scores (p<0.001). Off-target — extensively quantified and used as the specificity term. Across 114 GUIDE-seq guides (5,857,388 candidate off-target sites, 1,350 active at >=1% of on-target reads), guides were active at putative off-target sites with 0/1/2/3 mismatches in the "specificity-defining region" (SDR = guide positions 4-20 plus PAM) at rates of 100%, 43%, 5.7% and 0.4%; bulge-requiring sites showed no elevated activity. CFD score predicted the experimental off-target activity rate at Pearson R = 0.93. CRISPick Aggregate CFD (sum of CFD over off-target sites with <=1 SDR mismatch) classified promiscuous guides at macro-F1 0.77 in training (threshold 4.8) and 0.73 / 0.72 in DepMap Avana validation for TP53-wildtype (n=384) and TP53-mutant (n=623) cell lines, beating the GuideScan specificity score (F1 0.59 / 0.57, dropping to 0.43 / 0.41 at the recommended 0.2 threshold). 87.9% of off-target sites flagged by GuideScan are inactive vs 56.3% for Aggregate CFD. Jacquere excludes all guides with Aggregate CFD > 4.8 and deprioritizes (but tolerates) CFD = 1.0 perfect-match off-target sites.
4. **Data type**: Human cancer cell lines — A375 (melanoma) and A549 (lung) for the new Jacquere screens; A375 for the DeWeirdt 2022 training tiling data; ~1,007 DepMap cell lines (Avana 24Q2) stratified by TP53 status for validation. The GUIDE-seq benchmark corpus derives from primary human CD4+/CD8+ T cells, U2OS and HEK293. Library designs target the human (GRCh38) and mouse (GRCm38) genomes.

**Relevance flag**: This is the sequence-only, explicitly **cell-line-agnostic** baseline that CellGuide must extend — the authors state Jacquere "is cell-line agnostic for the sake of broad application" and include no chromatin/ATAC/epigenetic feature (chromatin appears only in a cited reference). Its direct contribution is a reusable **specificity term**: the SDR definition (positions 4-20 + PAM), CFD-sum aggregation restricted to <=1 SDR mismatch, the empirically calibrated promiscuity threshold of 4.8, and the GUIDE-seq mismatch-vs-activity rates (100/43/5.7/0.4%) that justify truncating the off-target search — all droppable into an efficiency x specificity formulation. Its only cross-context transferability claim is stability of the off-target classifier across TP53-wildtype vs mutant cell lines.
