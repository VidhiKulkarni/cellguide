# Agent 2 structured extraction — huang2024_dcas9_tcell_epigenetic

1. **Experimental design**: CRISPRa / epigenome editing (no nuclease, no DNA cut) using four dCas9 effector plasmids — epigenetic modifiers dCas9-p300 (Addgene 61357) and dCas9-TET1 (Addgene 167983) and transcriptional activators dCas9-VPH (Addgene 158091) and dCas9-VPR (Addgene 63798) — co-transfected with a pU6-sgRNA cassette (Addgene 53188). Delivery is plasmid transfection, not viral: 4D-Nucleofector nucleofection (program F1-115, 1000 ng total DNA) for PBMCs and THP-1-derived macrophages, Lipofectamine 3000 for HEK293FT. Assays: RT-qPCR, western blot, flow cytometry for TERT and CD3, ChIP-qPCR at the TERT promoter, TRAP telomerase activity assay, beta-galactosidase and p21 senescence assays, PI cell-cycle, CFDA-SE division tracking, Annexin V/PI viability, and karyotyping.
2. **Target genes**: A single endogenous gene — human TERT (hTERT) — targeted by three sgRNAs binding the TERT promoter at -157 bp, -333 bp and -499 bp from the TSS, each beginning with a 5-prime G for U6 transcription and followed by an NGG PAM. Readout/control genes: CD3 (T-cell marker), GAPDH (normalizer), GFP (transfection-efficiency reporter).
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — combined epigenetic modifiers raised TERT mRNA 2.65x in PBMCs, 53x in THP-1-derived macrophages and 1.87x in HEK293FT; adding the VPH/VPR activators gave 2.85x, 42.96x and 4.21x respectively. Single-effector tests exposed a strong cell-type dependence: dCas9-VPR and dCas9-p300 alone sufficed in HEK293FT (3.56x and 2.57x) but produced no TERT upregulation at all in PBMCs, while in M0 macrophages p300, TET1 and VPH alone gave 15.76x, 13.21x and 10.83x. TERT protein rose ~2.3-2.4-fold in PBMCs and ~1.3x in macrophages by western blot; TRAP telomerase activity rose 5.6-fold in PBMCs with all four effectors (3.4-fold with modifiers only) and ~1.24-1.32x in HEK293FT. Beta-galactosidase-positive senescent cells fell from 3.74% to 2.09% (PBMC), 13.62% to 7.79% (macrophage) and 25.24% to 4.94% (HEK293FT); CRISPRa-treated PBMCs achieved ~5.7 divisions per 240 h vs ~4.9 untreated and were expanded for at least three months. ChIP-qPCR showed effector-specific enrichment at the TERT promoter — H3K27ac, TET1, c-MYC and HSF1 in HEK293FT; TET1 enrichment in PBMCs — with no enrichment in controls. Off-target — essentially not assessed experimentally. Specificity was handled only at the design stage (CRISPOR via the UCSC CRISPR Targets track, and Cas-Designer/Cas-OFFinder, confirmed high genome-wide specificity, with no predicted off-target sites within 2-nt mismatches) and indirectly via safety endpoints: stable karyotype with no chromosomal abnormality after two months, no accelerated division (cells stayed in G1, unlike Jurkat), no malignant transformation, and normal viability. No genome-wide off-target transcriptome or binding assay (no RNA-seq, no ChIP-seq outside the TERT promoter) was performed.
4. **Data type**: Primary human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs, ATCC) cultured with IL-2, focusing on unstimulated/resting CD3+ T cells; THP-1 monocytic leukemia cells differentiated into M0 macrophages with 200 nM PMA; HEK293FT cells; Jurkat T-cell leukemia line used only as an immortalized comparator. No animal model.

**Relevance flag**: Directly relevant on the cell-context axis, weakly relevant on the guide-scoring axis. Its key contribution is a clean demonstration that the same three sgRNAs at the same promoter give radically different activation depending on the cell's chromatin state — no response to dCas9-VPR or dCas9-p300 alone in resting primary T cells versus 3.56x/2.57x in HEK293FT and 10-16x in macrophages — which is exactly the cross-cell-type non-transferability that motivates a context-aware efficiency term; the ChIP-qPCR H3K27ac/TET1 occupancy data also tie effector recruitment to a measurable epigenetic mark. It provides no per-guide efficiency series (only 3 guides, always pooled) and no quantitative off-target data usable as a specificity term.
