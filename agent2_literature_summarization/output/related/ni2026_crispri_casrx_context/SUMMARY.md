# Agent 2 structured extraction — ni2026_crispri_casrx_context

1. **Experimental design**: Head-to-head CRISPRi vs RNA-targeting CasRx knockdown benchmark, built on a KI platform: doxycycline-inducible (rtTA3G/TRE3G) ZIM3-dCas9 or RfxCas13d (CasRx), each P2A-mCherry and HA-tagged, knocked into the AAVS1 T2 safe-harbour locus of human PSCs; sgRNAs delivered by piggyBac transposon. Readouts: genotyping PCR, ddPCR copy number, G-banding, flow cytometry, western blot, immunocytochemistry, RT-qPCR, bulk RNA-seq, plus orthogonal siRNA knockdown and a 20-day induction/withdrawal time course.
2. **Target genes**: POU5F1/OCT4 (with NANOG as downstream readout), EIF2AK1 (reversibility time course), ATF5 and its overlapping-TSS neighbour NUP62, ESRG (HERV-H LTR7-driven TSS shared with RPL38L, IL34, KLKB1), circHIPK3 vs linear HIPK3, RAB6A-iso1 vs RAB6A-iso2, and CasRx-resistant circRNAs circFAT1, circAMBRA1, circCCDC134. Also references a prior whole-transcriptome CRISPRi essentiality screen in PSCs.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — CRISPRi gave 99.4% and CasRx 95.5% reduction of POU5F1 (both P < 0.0001), with NANOG down 88.9% and 82.9% respectively. CRISPRi reached >99.9% EIF2AK1 repression by day 1 and needed until ~day 20 after Dox withdrawal to recover; CasRx reached only >90% by day 3 but fully recovered 1 day after withdrawal. CasRx achieved >99.9% ATF5 knockdown, 88.7% ESRG knockdown (P < 0.005), >99.9% circHIPK3 knockdown without touching linear HIPK3, and 76% / 53% knockdown of RAB6A-iso1 / iso2; three circRNAs were essentially resistant. Cas expression itself perturbed the endogenous transcriptome only minimally (RNA-seq, |log2FC| > 1, adj. P < 0.05). Off-target — the headline off-target result is CRISPRi positional/chromatin off-target, not spacer mismatch: sgATF5 co-repressed NUP62 by 99.3% (P < 0.0001) because the two genes share an overlapping TSS, and orthogonal siRNA showed the lethal phenotype attributed to ATF5 actually came from NUP62. CasRx knocked down ATF5 with no NUP62 effect, and targeted ESRG without affecting the three genes sharing its HERV-H LTR7 TSS. The authors compiled a list of additional overlapping-TSS off-target gene pairs from public annotation (Table S1, not in saved text) and recommend vetting targets against it plus known retrotransposon TSSs.
4. **Data type**: Human pluripotent stem cells — parental line WTB6, with 1B4 (CRISPRi Gen 1 clone 4, derived from WTB6, single AAVS1 KI) as the copy-number reference; all engineered lines carried two KI alleles. No animal model or primary tissue.

**Relevance flag**: Relevant mainly as a mechanistic argument that for CRISPRi the specificity term must include a genomic/chromatin-architecture component: because dCas9-KRAB acts through TSS-proximal chromatin spreading, guides at loci with overlapping or shared TSSs (ATF5-NUP62; HERV-H LTR7-driven TSSs) cause quantified collateral repression (99.3% off-target knockdown of NUP62) that no spacer-mismatch score would flag, and the overlapping-TSS gene-pair list is a directly usable penalty feature. It offers no chromatin accessibility/ATAC measurements, no cross-cell-type comparison, and no SpCas9 nuclease cutting or indel data, so it is only tangentially relevant to the nuclease-KO efficiency term.
