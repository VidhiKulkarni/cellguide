# Agent 2 structured extraction — taifour2026_dcas9_locus_silencing

1. **Experimental design**: CRISPRi-style epigenetic repression with SpdCas9-KRAB (catalytically dead, no DNA cleavage), delivered two ways — (a) lentiviral pLV vector co-expressing dCas9-KRAB and gRNA, and (b) purified NLS-FLAG-E20-dCas9-KRAB protein plus synthetic gRNAs as ribonucleoprotein (RNP) complexes packaged in a generation-5 PAMAM dendronized polymer functionalized with p-guanidinobenzoic acid (GBA-polymer) for non-viral in vitro and intratumoral in vivo delivery. Readouts: qRT-PCR, western blot, immunofluorescence, bulk RNA-seq, soft-agar clonogenicity, scratch wound-healing, Ki-67 staining, and tumor volume in xenograft/PDX mice.
2. **Target genes**: The EWSR1-FLI1 fusion oncogene, addressed via six gRNAs tiled across the EWSR1 proximal promoter and 5' UTR (positions numbered relative to the TSS of transcript NM_005243.4); the three most active (gRNAs 2, 3, 5) were pooled as a "gRNA mix". Sixteen computationally predicted off-target promoters were assayed (Table S2; individual symbols not listed in the saved text). Control guides targeted GFP. RNA-seq named downstream regulated genes including CXCL12, IL-15, IL7R, IL13R2, TNFSF4, CD274/PD-L1, HLA-B, TAP1, TAP2, ERAP2, PARP9, TREX1, ERCC6, CDK6 and CCND1.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — single-guide repression of EWSR1-FLI1 mRNA to 0.39 / 0.26 / 0.12-fold (gRNAs 2 / 3 / 5) in A673 and 0.59 / 0.58 / 0.10-fold in RD-ES relative to dCas9-KRAB with no gRNA; the 3-guide mix outperformed every single guide and the 6-guide pool (0.044-fold in RD-ES; 0.15-fold for the 6-guide pool in A673), with near-complete protein loss on western blot/IF. Polymer-delivered dCas9-KRAB RNP repressed EWS-FLI1 protein by 48% at 24 h in A673. In vivo, lentiviral gRNA-mix xenografts were >50% smaller at day 15 (155 mm3 vs 432 mm3 no-gRNA and 601 mm3 non-transduced); RNP-treated A673 xenografts were 1.5–1.6-fold smaller by day 8; ES-4 PDX tumors ~40–50% smaller (136 vs 252 mm3 at day 38), with effects fading after ~12 days as dCas9 expression decayed. Off-target — addressed but narrowly: guide sequences with three or fewer mismatches were computationally mapped to the proximal promoters of sixteen putative off-target genes, and qRT-PCR in A673 showed no significant deregulation of any of the sixteen (Figure S1). Bulk RNA-seq found 4,557 DEGs (1,863 down / 2,694 up) vs no-gRNA control, but these are interpreted as downstream EWS-FLI1 network effects (validated by GSEA against published EWS-FLI1 shRNA-knockdown and ectopic-expression signatures), not direct dCas9 off-targets. No genome-wide binding assay (ChIP-seq / GUIDE-seq) was performed. A nonspecific GFP-targeting gRNA RNP produced no tumor growth change.
4. **Data type**: Human Ewing sarcoma cell lines A673 and RD-ES (both EWSR1-FLI1 translocation-positive); primary human dermal fibroblasts (HDFa) as a non-transformed comparator; NOD-SCID-IL2Rgamma-null (NSG) mouse subcutaneous xenografts of A673; and the ES-4 Ewing sarcoma patient-derived xenograft (PDX). Recombinant dCas9-KRAB protein produced in E. coli.

**Relevance flag**: Moderately relevant. It supplies a tiled-guide efficiency series at a single promoter (six guides, TSS-relative position vs measured knockdown fold) plus a concrete, cheap specificity readout — enumerate <=3-mismatch genomic matches, then qRT-PCR the 16 predicted off-target promoters — which maps directly onto a mismatch-count-based specificity term. Its chromatin content is mechanistic rather than predictive (KRAB/KAP1 recruiting SETDB1/HP1alpha/NuRD, losing H3K27ac/H3K9ac and gaining H3K9me3/H3K27me3); there is no ATAC/accessibility feature and no cross-cell-type accessibility contrast, so it contributes no cross-context transferability data.
