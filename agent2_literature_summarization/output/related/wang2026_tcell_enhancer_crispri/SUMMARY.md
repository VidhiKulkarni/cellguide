# Agent 2 structured extraction — wang2026_tcell_enhancer_crispri

1. **Experimental design**: CRISPRi (primary modality) plus Cas9 nuclease indel saturation mutagenesis and CRISPRa, delivered by an engineered **all-in-one lentiviral vector** carrying both the sgRNA and dCas9-ZIM3(KRAB) under bidirectional promoters with a GFP (or Thy1.1) marker; TIL validation used Cas9-sgRNA **RNP electroporation**. Assay is a pooled FACS-based screen: cells sorted into top vs bottom 20% bins of target protein by flow cytometry, sgRNA abundance read out by deep sequencing, "CRISPRi score" = log2FC(bottom/top). Two library designs were run head-to-head — an **ATAC-seq-guided accessible-chromatin-region (ACR) library** (4-6 sgRNAs per ACR) and an unbiased **tiled library at 200 bp intervals** spanning ~200-335 kb, analyzed with a 20-sgRNA sliding window.
2. **Target genes**: Non-coding cis-regulatory elements (CREs) at four loci — **CXCR3** (platform pilot: 231-sgRNA ACR library over 17 ACRs; 901-sgRNA tiling library over ~335 kb), **PDCD1** (PD-1), **HAVCR2** (TIM-3), and **TBX21** (TBET; 350-sgRNA ACR library over 40 ACRs across 213 kb). Cas9-indel saturation-mutagenesis libraries (~190 sgRNAs each) tiled the newly found PDCD1 and HAVCR2 CREs. Also assayed: **STAT5B** (CRISPRi knockdown validating a predicted TF motif) and downstream readouts **GZMB** and **PRF1** (Perforin). CAR construct: anti-GD2.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — the all-in-one design raised CRISPRi transduction efficiency from 12.2% (conventional dual-vector) to **33.3%**, and ZIM3-KRAB raised CXCR3 silencing from ~69% to **~85%** vs wild-type ZNF10-KRAB. Screens recovered the known CXCR3 -4.1 kb enhancer and found new +1.6 kb and +2.3 kb CREs; new CREs at PDCD1 **+14.8 kb and +16.1 kb** (15-25% PD-1 surface reduction vs ~50% for a promoter sgRNA) and HAVCR2 **-33 kb, +78.5 kb, +84 kb** (25-50% TIM-3 reduction vs 75% for TSS sgRNAs); TBX21 CREs at -12.3/-13.3/-13.7 kb. Minimal-promoter GFP reporters confirmed enhancer activity (2.5-6-fold GFP increase; PDCD1 +16.1 kb ~2.6-fold). Cas9-indel saturation mutagenesis resolved essential sequences PES1-9 and HES1-3; disrupting the single HES2 element gave a **~50-60% TIM-3 reduction, statistically indistinguishable from CRISPRi silencing of the entire +84 kb CRE**. Combinatorial CRISPRi (2-3 vectors/cell) was additive: three PDCD1 and seven HAVCR2 enhancer pairs, and two triple combinations (HEN1-HEN2-HEN4, HEN1-HEN3-HEN4), approached TSS-level repression. Functionally, HES2-edited anti-GD2 CAR T cells retained ~50% tumor killing at day 8 where control and full TIM-3 KO CAR T cells had lost tumor control, and produced more IFN-gamma/GM-CSF than full KO. In melanoma TIL-derived CD8 T cells, RNP targeting of PES2 reduced PD-1 by **18%** and HES2 reduced TIM-3 by **41%**, matching healthy-donor effect sizes. Off-target — **not addressed experimentally**; no GUIDE-seq/CIRCLE-seq/Cas-OFFinder analysis and no off-target indel quantification is reported. Off-target effects appear only as Discussion-level rationale (large DNA edits carry "inherent risks due to off-target effects and potential chromosomal aberrations especially in settings of multiple edits", motivating base/prime editors and epigenome silencers). The nearest empirical specificity controls are non-targeting sgRNAs, sgRNAs targeting non-accessible unrelated genomic regions, and sgRNAs targeting 100 bp flanks on either side of each enhancer — all centered on a CRISPRi score of 0 (background range -1 to +1).
4. **Data type**: **Primary human CD8 T cells** from healthy-donor PBMCs (2 donors per screen, 3-5 donors for validation; donor IDs ND584, ND539), activated in vitro; **melanoma tumor-infiltrating lymphocyte (TIL)-derived CD8 T cells** expanded with irradiated PBMC feeders + high-dose IL-2; anti-GD2 CAR T cells co-cultured with **143B osteosarcoma** tumor cells (GFP+). Chronic-stimulation exhaustion model = tonic-signaling anti-GD2 CAR, CD8+CD4 co-culture out to day 31. No immortalized cell lines used for the screens; no animal model.

**Relevance flag**: Highly relevant — arguably the most on-point paper for CellGuide's premise. It runs an explicit **ATAC-seq-guided (ACR) sgRNA library against a chromatin-agnostic 200 bp tiling library in the same cells**, exactly the comparison a chromatin-context-aware metric claims to win, and it reports a concrete failure mode of naive accessibility priors: the functional CXCR3 +2.3 kb CRE was *excluded* from the ACR library because that region had variable accessibility across donors in the reference ATAC-seq dataset, yet scored highly in the unbiased tiling screen in both donors. It also supplies a directly usable notion of cell-state-dependent guide efficacy: the same HAVCR2 enhancer guides change potency as chromatin remodels during exhaustion (+84 kb CRE goes from ~40% TIM-3 reduction at day 10 to ~70% at day 31; the -33 kb and +89 kb CREs show no activity before day 28), so the "best" guide depends on differentiation state, not sequence alone. Cell type (primary human T cells) matches the Ito et al. benchmark context. Caveat for the specificity term: this paper contributes **nothing quantitative on off-targets**, and its per-guide scores are CRE-repression log2FCs, not coding-exon indel rates, so they are not directly comparable to Ito's per-guide indel %.
