# Agent 2 structured extraction — zhang2025_crispri_k562_screens

1. **Experimental design**: CRISPRi knockdown (no DNA cutting), delivered lentivirally as a modified CROP-seq ("CRISPRi-CROP-seq"). K562 cells were first transduced with pHR-UCOE-SFFV-dCas9-mCherry-ZIM3-KRAB (Addgene #154473) at MOI ~0.5 and mCherry-sorted, then transduced at low MOI with a pooled sgRNA library carrying the sgRNA E+F scaffold (A-U flip plus 5 bp hairpin extension); double-positive (TagBFP/mCherry) cells were FACS-sorted at D3 and profiled at D7 on the 10x Genomics Single Cell 5-prime CRISPR Screening Assay, which directly captures sgRNAs from the cDNA library without a separate enrichment step. Validation was arrayed CRISPRi plus RT-qPCR; analysis used Mixscape, SCENIC (regulon activity RAC / regulon specificity RSS), and reanalysis of two published CRISPRi Perturb-seq datasets.
2. **Target genes**: Seven transcription factors, 3 sgRNAs each from the Dolcetto library plus 5 non-targeting sgRNAs (26 sgRNAs total; 21 assigned, 18 targeting): LMO2, TCF3, LDB1, MYB, GATA2, RUNX1, and FLI1 as a negative control (not expressed in K562). Reanalyzed public datasets: Wu et al. 2022 (480 sgRNAs / 120 coding genes plus 120 lncRNAs in iPSCs; 240 coding-gene sgRNAs retained) and Replogle et al. 2022 (384 sgRNAs / 128 genes in K562).
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — knockdown efficacy per sgRNA, measured as target-gene log2FC in scRNA-seq and as Mixscape-called perturbed-cell fraction. 10,848 cells recovered; 6,929 (63.9%) with exactly one sgRNA; median 3,535 sgRNA UMIs/cell; median 4,623 genes/cell. Only 4 of 18 targeting sgRNAs (MYB-1, MYB-2, MYB-3, LMO2-3) reached log2FC < -1; Mixscape called perturbation for 15/21 sgRNAs but with extreme within-gene variability (GATA2 sgRNAs ranged from 5% to 90% of cells perturbed, while MYB sgRNAs were uniformly effective); TCF3 sgRNAs were expressed and captured yet produced no detectable perturbation. By differential expression only 6 of 18 sgRNAs had a significant impact, and 3 of 6 targeted TFs (MYB, GATA2, LMO2) were successfully knocked down — roughly a 50% success rate. DEG counts: 914 (MYB), 1,364 (LMO2), 236 (GATA2), validated by RT-qPCR (MYB up: HBE1, HBZ, ALAS2, GYPB, HBA2; MYB down: NMU, RP11-301G19.1, PDE4DIP, KCNH2, FAM178B) and by MYB/GATA2 ChIP-seq occupancy at TSS plus/minus 5 kb. GRN reconstruction from 118 SCENIC regulons yielded 23 key regulons, recovering LMO2-GATA2, MYB-MYC, MYB-GATA2, GATA2-KLF1, LMO2-MYC. Reanalysis of public data: 133/240 (iPSC) and 185/377 (K562) sgRNAs passed the fold-change threshold (~50%); requiring both fold-change and Mixscape agreement dropped this to 97/240 (iPSC) and 56/377 (K562), substantially lower than originally reported. Off-target — genome-wide off-target editing is not assayed (CRISPRi, no nuclease). The specificity analysis is transcriptional only: SCENIC regulon-specific score (RSS) per knockdown cluster, plus non-targeting sgRNAs and the FLI1 negative control confirming absence of a perturbation signature. The authors flag one likely false-positive inferred edge (GATA2 activating GATA1, inconsistent with the GATA switch), attributed to SCENIC over-sensitivity rather than guide off-target activity.
4. **Data type**: Human K562 chronic myeloid leukemia (erythroleukemic) cell line for all primary experiments. Reanalyzed public data adds human iPSCs (Wu et al.) and further K562 (Replogle et al.). No primary cells, animal model, or tissue.

**Relevance flag**: Directly relevant, and unusually well matched to CellGuide because K562 is the Ito et al. 2024 cross-context comparator line. It supplies a hard quantitative prior for the efficiency term — only roughly 25-50% of library-designed sgRNAs produce effective knockdown (4/18 by log2FC here; 97/240 and 56/377 in reanalyzed public data), with large variability between guides against the same gene despite equal delivery, sgRNA expression, and capture. Crucially, the Discussion attributes the residual failure to guide-intrinsic design factors including the sgRNA ability to bind target promoters, which it states may be influenced by the local chromatin state, and recommends future sgRNA design that takes local chromatin state into account — a direct, citable motivation for a chromatin-context-aware efficiency score. It provides no mismatch/off-target data usable as a specificity term.
