CellGuide Bench

Ranks CRISPR guide RNAs by sequence efficacy and chromatin accessibility — benchmarked against Ito et al. 2024's primary T-cell dataset, with a built-in skeptic that keeps the score honest. Does not assess off-target risk / specificity — see the warning banner below.

⚠ 3 things to know before trusting these numbers
  • No off-target/specificity assessment — tried, no real data source, removed (see agent3_metric_construction/SPEC.md).
  • Single-paper validation — ρ=0.441 is against Ito et al. 2024 only (n=199), no independent replication; some thresholds are derived from this same data.
  • Every score is source-labeled — real tool, real model, or fallback heuristic (the heuristic alone has no real signal, ρ=0.043, n.s.) — treat unlabeled/heuristic scores as placeholders.
Headline finding

The combined score currently underperforms its own sequence-only component

On 199 real Ito et al. 2024 guides, blending in accessibility at the current untuned weights (0.4 / 0.3) makes ranking worse, not better, than sequence alone (ρ 0.315 vs 0.441) — which is why recommended_score is sequence-only, with accessibility checked separately as a gate. See Skeptic Review for the full audit.

    Literature corpus

    waiting on Agent 1 — Literature search…

    Cross-paper synthesis. Sequence-only guide scores do not transfer across cell contexts, and chromatin accessibility is the most reusable context feature found in the corpus — but only conditionally (see the Guide Tracks tab): it adds real signal among guides that already have a decent sequence score, not as a standalone bonus. A specificity/off-target term was also motivated by this corpus (the same open-chromatin regions that raise on-target efficiency can also raise off-target risk) but was never built — no off-target dataset in the corpus was directly usable without API access we don't have. This tool does not assess off-target risk.
