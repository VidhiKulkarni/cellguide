# Agent 2 structured extraction — Xu et al. 2018

1. **Experimental design**: KO (RNP cleavage) + KI (HDR via PAM-disrupting ssODN), delivered via a "tube electroporation" device.
2. **Target genes**: B2M, APP, AAVS1, OCT4, PDCD1.
3. **Outcomes**: On-target — editing efficiency consistent across cell types (<30% variance) under efficient RNP delivery, contradicting prior reports of 4–10× cross-cell-type differences; the paper argues those earlier differences were largely a delivery artifact, not biological. HDR rates up to 42%. Off-target — not reported.
4. **Data type**: HEK293FT, primary human MSC, human iPSC, primary human T cells.
